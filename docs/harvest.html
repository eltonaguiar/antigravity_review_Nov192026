HARVEST — analyst endorsement signals

Sell-side analyst consensus per symbol, from a licensed data feed. Sortable and filterable by date and asset class. Not investment advice.

Every column is defined in the legend below it. The full method — endpoints, formulas, coverage semantics, known weaknesses and a reviewer checklist — is in HARVEST_METHODOLOGY.md (and the per-class universe/coverage plan in harvest/UNIVERSE.md), written to be read by both people and language models.

Read this in 30 seconds.
  • One row = one stock. “Analysts” is how many professionals cover it. “Buy-side %” is the share saying Buy or Strong Buy.
  • Trend compares this month to last month — it is not an upgrade or downgrade event.
  • A blank / 0 analysts is not “bad”. It means the data feed publishes no analyst coverage for that instrument type (ETFs, bonds, crypto) or our plan cannot access it (mutual funds).
  • This is an opinion poll, not advice — and we have not yet measured whether these analysts’ calls made money. That track-record layer is the next build.

Quick views:
How to read this table — every column, in plain English
SB · B · H · S · SS
The count of analysts in each rating bucket for the latest month: Strong Buy, Buy, Hold, Sell, Strong Sell. A cell reading 19 / 48 / 3 / 0 / 0 means 19 analysts rated Strong Buy, 48 Buy, 3 Hold, none Sell, none Strong Sell. The five numbers add up to the Analysts column.
Analysts
Total number of covering analysts — the sum of the five buckets. Always read this before the percentage columns: a row backed by 70 analysts means far more than one backed by 3.
Consensus

One label, derived only from the five counts — not a separate opinion. Each analyst scores +2 (SB), +1 (B), 0 (H), −1 (S), −2 (SS).

score = (2·SB + B − S − 2·SS) / analysts · buy-side = (SB + B) / analysts

  • STRONG BUY: score ≥ 1.0 and buy-side ≥ 75%
  • BUY: score ≥ 0.3 and buy-side ≥ 50%
  • SELL: score ≤ -0.3 and buy-side ≤ 30%
  • STRONG SELL: score ≤ -1.0 and buy-side ≤ 15%
  • HOLD: anything else.

Why both conditions? A score alone can label a split name BUY; INTC scores 0.35 but only 29% of analysts say Buy, so it is HOLD.

Buy-side %
Share of analysts who are positive: (SB + B) ÷ Analysts. 67.7% means roughly two thirds of covering analysts rate the name Buy or Strong Buy. It deliberately ignores how strong — which is why the Consensus label sits next to it.
MoM pp
Change in Buy-side % versus the prior month, in percentage points. +1.8 means the buy-side share rose 1.8 points month over month. A value of 0.0 means no change once rounded, not missing data.
Trend

Computed once in the pipeline and stored; the page does not re-derive it. IMPROVING/DETERIORATING requires both:

  • |Δ buy-side| ≥ max(2.0 pp, 100 / n_now) — the coverage-relative noise floor
  • at least 2 analysts changed rating bucket

Otherwise STABLE. If the analyst count changed between months the row is tagged CHURN_CONTAMINATED. This is a change in the analyst mix, not a firm upgrade/downgrade event.

Snapshot period
Shown once above the table; every row shares it. The recency filter and per-row period/age are hidden until the feed spans more than one month.
Summary cards above
Symbols covered — rows with at least one month of consensus. Avg buy-side — the mean of Buy-side % across those rows. Improving / Deteriorating — counts of rows in each Trend bucket. Net trend — Improving minus Deteriorating, a count of symbols, not a percentage. Generated — UTC time this snapshot was built.
