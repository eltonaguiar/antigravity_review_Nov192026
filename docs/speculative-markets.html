1. Penny stocks — NOT RELIABLE

ELI5: Sub-$1 shares look cheap but are usually illiquid, heavily diluted, and prone to pumps. The quoted price is not a price you can actually trade at size.
  • No local bar series for OTC/penny names; we cannot backtest them honestly.
  • Ledger evidence: **PENNY_STOCK** 4 resolved picks, WR **50%**, PF **0.1779** — a single big loss wipes the wins.
  • Missing: point-in-time prices, delisting/suspension history, borrow/dilution data, realistic spread and capacity.
  • Verdict: not reliably backtestable; do not allocate. A $50 fill can move the price.

What would change these verdicts

  • Penny: point-in-time OTC bars + delisting + spread/capacity data; until then, no.
  • Meme: a pre-registered rule with forward n≥100, net CI-low>0, DSR≥0.95; current evidence is negative.
  • Options: historical IV surfaces, greeks, and fill data; a snapshot cannot support a claim.
  • Futures: roll-adjusted continuous contracts + COT + costs; then re-rank with CPCV/DSR.
