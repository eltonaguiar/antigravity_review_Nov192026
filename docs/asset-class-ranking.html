4. Why consensus is not an edge

Three separate objections, each sufficient on its own:

  • It is one bet, not fifteen. Thirteen of the fifteen are long. SPY, VTSAX and VFIAX are the same underlying in three wrappers; IEF and TLT are the same duration trade at two lengths; AAPL, NVDA and BRK.B are equity beta. Traded as a book this is a single long-risk-asset factor, and a factor cannot diversify itself.
  • Agreement between models trained on overlapping text is not independent evidence. Ten of eighteen long SPY is consistent with a shared prior — US equities go up — rather than ten independent measurements. The contest's own bootstrap agrees: the best entrant wins 41.6% of 2,000 replicates, far below the 95% needed for a distinguishable ranking, with only four picks per entrant.
  • Our measured evidence actively contradicts two of the top three. CRYPTO is a class-level KILL and COMMODITY is a class-level KILL, yet BTCUSD-long is the second-strongest consensus and XAUUSD-long is on the list. Consensus that contradicts measurement is a bias to be discounted, not a signal to be followed.

Forward scoring is wired to the repo's own executor (CONTEST/tools/assess_contest.py), which ignores everything an agent reports about itself: entry is the first bar's open at or after the round start, exit is the first touched barrier, and on an ambiguous bar the stop wins.

Why we are not printing a performance table anyway. With 4 of 24 symbols covered, a per-class or per-tool return column would be computed almost entirely from FX and gold. It would look like measurement and would be a coverage artefact. The tracker publishes the coverage gap first, because that is the honest headline until the bars exist.

6. What would change the answer

  • Forward bars for the 20 missing symbols. This unblocks per-class and per-tool scoring and is the single highest-value item; nothing else can be measured without it.
  • More picks per entrant. At four picks the bootstrap cannot separate entrants. Roughly thirty would make the ranking decidable — or prove it is not.
  • A cell that survives deflated Sharpe. One cell already produced n=58, +50.51 bp net and permutation p=0.001, and died at DSR 0.0031. That gap between permutation p and DSR is the whole lesson: with 528 trials behind us, a raw p-value is not evidence.
  • Cost reality. Charge the real round trip, every leg, every rebalance. In the bond test the sign flipped from +84 bp to −91.58 bp on that one change alone.
