## Diagnosis — why there are no reliable profitable picks

1. **Multiple testing without correction, gates disabled.** 658 strategies,
   FDR/Bonferroni gates off on the live surface; ~33 expected false positives;
   every "winner" is in the single-source artifact cluster.
2. **Wrong win definition.** WR>50% gate is a break-even test for symmetric
   payoffs; trend/premia cells with WR~35% / RR~2.5 can never pass; TP-first
   labeling produces "high WR, PF<1" (EQUITY 59-60% WR, PF 0.94).
3. **Exits destroy edge.** Crypto LONG −0.44%/pick vs holding the same coin;
   median TP +2.50% / SL −1.50% cuts winners short. No A/B harness exists to
   test the counterfactual (the `add-strategy-variable-tracking` branch is empty).
4. **Data debt.** 22,947 ghost rows vs hard-coded 0/GREEN; 94,119 open vs 21
   active; 27% magnitude mismatch; `fx_prices` stale since 2026-02-09 (211 forex
   picks unmeasurable); all 285 `bt_backtest_runs` mistagged CRYPTO; backtests
   stale since 2026-03-06; 76M-row `at_local_picks` mirror; 165 retired-strategy
   picks still emitting.
5. **No forward-observation ledger.** `PROVEN` can never be minted from live
   data; every winner claim is backward-looking and, in several places,
   contradictory (ETF WR 1.9% on `/audit` vs 43.1% on `/audit3`).
6. **Kill tribunal is advisory.** Its output is a web page, not a denylist the
   scanner reads — 165 retired-strategy picks still emit.

## Recommended next move

Pre-register **one** pod (CRYPTO long, ensemble grade ∈ {A,B}, score ≥ 60,
symmetric vol-scaled exit) and forward-shadow it 90 days with a same-symbol
hold benchmark leg, while (a) stamping `at_issue_*` for 100% of rows, (b)
backfilling FX or retiring it, and (c) wiring the kill tribunal as the
scanner's denylist. Decision rule at day 90: if no pod shows positive net
CI-LB at 2× slippage and IR ≥ 0.5, declare "no deployable edge on available
data" — a successful, citable result, and freeze (not delete) the harness.
